# 📊 End-to-End HR Analytics Dashboard

### Employee Attrition & Performance Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project delivers a comprehensive **HR Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze employee attrition, performance, compensation, and workforce demographics.

The solution integrates **SQL, Python (Pandas), and Power BI** to transform raw HR data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To identify:

* 🔴 Key drivers of employee attrition
* 📉 High-risk employee segments
* 💰 Compensation gaps across roles
* 😊 Employee satisfaction & engagement patterns

## 📈 Key Insights

* 🔴 Employees with **low satisfaction & overtime** are most likely to leave
* ⏳ Highest attrition occurs within **first 2 years of employment**
* 💰 Lower salary bands show **higher attrition risk**
* 👨‍🔬 Specific job roles (e.g., Sales Executive, Lab Technician) drive attrition
* ⚖️ Work-life balance strongly impacts retention

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Improve onboarding & engagement for early-tenure employees
* Reduce overtime workload in high-risk departments
* Introduce compensation adjustments for low-income roles
* Enhance employee satisfaction programs
